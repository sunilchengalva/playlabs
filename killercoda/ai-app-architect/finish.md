# Demo Complete — AI App Architect

## What just happened

You gave Claude a one-line product brief. It produced:

- A structured **user flow** in 5–6 steps
- A complete list of **key screens** with descriptions
- A **component architecture** for the frontend
- A **database schema** with tables and relationships

All in under 20 seconds.

## The Engineering 5.0 lesson

This is not automation. This is **collaboration**.

The AI handled the scaffolding — the parts that follow patterns, that require breadth but not depth, that benefit from scale.

The engineer handled the judgment — the brief itself, the evaluation of the output, the decision about what fits the actual context, the responsibility for what ships.

That division of labour is what Engineering 5.0 looks like in practice.

## What to ask your students

If you use a demo like this in your classroom, the question that unlocks learning is not *"what did the AI produce?"* — it is:

> *"What would you change, and why?"*

That question tests domain knowledge, critical thinking, and the ability to evaluate AI outputs. It is a better test of engineering skill than asking them to produce the same output from scratch.
